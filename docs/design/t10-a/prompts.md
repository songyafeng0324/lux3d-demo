## 01 入口主视角

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: architectural concept art for a browser 3D treasure gallery, landscape 16:9. Create one beautiful, plausible, buildable Chinese xianxia timber gallery interior, sophisticated cinematic game environment visualization. Exactly TWO occupiable floors: ground and one upper gallery, no third floor. Rectangular hall 24m wide and 28m deep; upper floor 4.8m above ground, clear central atrium opening 12m wide and 12m deep. Camera at human eye height 1.65m near the SOUTH entrance looking NORTH, centered, moderately wide 26mm perspective, no fisheye, floor visible. A single coherent U-shaped return staircase sits at the far NORTH center behind the atrium: two side-by-side flights, first rises away on the right to a half-height rear landing, second rises back toward viewer on the left to the upper north gallery. Show stairs visibly, believable rails and landing, no ceremonial throne or altar. Continuous upper timber gallery surrounds the atrium with protective wooden balustrades. Four to six substantial dark timber columns frame a powerful vertical view, upper beams support a pitched Chinese roof with a diffuse daylight roof lantern, not an additional floor. Display pedestals occupy the side galleries, a few jade vessels, bronze vessels and ceramic artifacts as conceptual placeholder exhibits; leave the central axis wide and uncluttered. Deep aged brown timber, restrained antique brass joinery and dark muted red accents, grey stone ground floor, timber upper floor. Cool pale skylight and pools of warm amber lantern light, readable shadows and believable material roughness. Refined quiet ancient treasure pavilion, slightly mysterious, architectural grandeur without excessive palace ornament. A modest lantern centered high above the atrium is the focal glow, never hiding the stairs. Essential structure and broad materials are emphasized, restrained simple geometric panels, no intricate all-over carvings, no huge drapery. No people, no text, no logos, no UI, no watermark. Deliver a coherent single scene, not a collage.
